The Eiffel Tower is a tall, iron tower in Paris, France. It was built by Gustave Eiffel for a big fair in 1889. At first, some people didn't like it because it looked too industrial, but it quickly became very popular. For a long time, it was the tallest man-made structure in the world.

You can go up the tower to three different levels to see amazing views of Paris. From the top, you can spot famous places like the Arc de Triomphe. At night, the tower itself sparkles with lights every hour, which is beautiful to watch.

The Eiffel Tower isn't just for looking at. It's also been used for things like radio broadcasts. Today, it's a global symbol and one of the most famous landmarks in the world.
