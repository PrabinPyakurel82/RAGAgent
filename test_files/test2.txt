The Great Wall of China is a very long wall built in China. It's not just one wall, but many sections built over many years by different Chinese emperors. The main reason it was built was to protect China from invaders from the north.

The wall stretches for thousands of miles across mountains, deserts, and grasslands. It's so long that it's often called the "Long Wall." People can walk on parts of it, and it's amazing to see how it winds through the landscape.

Today, the Great Wall is a famous symbol of China and a UNESCO World Heritage site. It shows the incredible effort and skill of the ancient Chinese people. It's one of the most popular tourist attractions in the world.
